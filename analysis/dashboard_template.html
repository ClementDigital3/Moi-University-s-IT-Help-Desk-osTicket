A context-aware machine learning model for ICT help desk ticket management: a case study of Moi University. Every figure below is read from the result files produced by the modelling pipeline.
Data provenance. These results come from a corpus constructed to the field specification of Table 3.1 and Appendix II, standing in for the authorised osTicket export pending the data-access approval described in Sections 1.10 and 3.16. The corpus reproduces the conditions the study is about — symptom vocabulary shared across unrelated categories, disambiguating detail supplied only sometimes — so the comparative and methodological findings are informative. The absolute performance levels are properties of this corpus and are to be re-estimated on institutional data before any operational claim is made.
